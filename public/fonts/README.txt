Avenir (Regular, Medium, Heavy) for the entire site, including the M'ARCHVE wordmark.

1. Obtain Avenir from your brand kit, Monotype, or Adobe Fonts
   (you need a web embedding license).

2. Export or convert to WOFF2 and save as:
   Avenir-Roman.woff2   (weight 400)
   Avenir-Medium.woff2  (weight 500, optional)
   Avenir-Heavy.woff2   (weight 700)

3. Place those files in this folder (public/fonts/).

Until the files are here, the site uses system-installed Avenir on macOS
and falls back to Helvetica Neue / Arial on other platforms.

The logo uses Unicode U+2019 (RIGHT SINGLE QUOTATION MARK), not the
straight keyboard apostrophe (U+0027).
